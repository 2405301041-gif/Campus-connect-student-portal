Eligibility & Code of Conduct

school Who can register?

Open to all verified college undergraduates, graduate students, and high-school seniors (18+) enrolled in an accredited academic institution. Valid .edu email or student ID required at check-in.

group_add Team Size & Composition

Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members. Solo hackers are encouraged to join the team formation mixer on Friday at 4:00 PM or connect via our Discord matching lounge prior to the sprint.

terminal Code Originality

All hackathon code, assets, and design schemas must be written freshly during the 36-hour window. Utilizing open-source libraries, public frameworks, and pre-trained LLM models is warmly permitted.
