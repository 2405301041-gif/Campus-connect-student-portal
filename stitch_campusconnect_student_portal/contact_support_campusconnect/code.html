Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers regarding digital ticketing, event admittance policies, club registration credentials, and graduation credits.

Once you RSVP or purchase entry to any approved campus event via the Events tab, your unique cryptographic QR pass generates immediately inside your Dashboard > My Passes. You will also receive an authenticated copy delivered straight to your verified student inbox with Google Wallet and Apple Wallet pass links.
Open registration policy depends on the hosting society. Large-scale invitationals such as the Annual Hackathon and Spring Cultural Fest allow registered non-collegiate guests, provided they submit government photo identification during checkout. Inter-collegiate attendees must enter their active university email for automatic verification.
Chartering a collegiate organization requires: (1) A core executive team of at least 4 enrolled students in good academic standing, (2) One full-time faculty advisor confirmation letter, (3) A minimum of 15 student co-signers, and (4) Submission of an official constitution using the Student Affairs Club Charter template. Approvals take 5-7 business days.
All official events tagged with Co-Curricular Point Recognition sync attendance stamps through the CampusConnect scanner kiosks at check-in. These points reflect on your official Degree Audit transcript under Tier-2 Extracurricular Competencies within 48 hours of event completion.
